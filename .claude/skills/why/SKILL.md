# Breaking down a phenomenon

1. Facts: what happened? Confirm the moves in prices, rates or volumes with data, with source and date. If something cannot be found, say so.
2. Mechanism: write the transmission chain 「A → B → C」 and mark each link as fact or inference.
3. Competing explanations: at least two, each with the evidence that would refute it.
4. Historical analogues: 1–2 similar periods, comparing what followed over 3 / 6 / 12 months with data, with a warning that the sample is small.
5. What it means for me: against the IPS, is any rule triggered? Most of the time the answer is 「不需要行动」 (no action needed).
6. Signals worth tracking: 2–3 indicators that can be observed from here on.
Not allowed: forecasting price levels or giving buy or sell advice.
